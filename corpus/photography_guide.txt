# CreativeSync Freelance Photography Studio Guide

## Outdoor Golden Hour & Sunset Photography Gear
For outdoor sunset shoots, key gear includes:
- Camera Body: Full-frame mirrorless (e.g. Sony A7IV or Canon R6)
- Lenses: 85mm f/1.4 prime for portraits, 24-70mm f/2.8 zoom for versatile environmental framing.
- Lighting: Portable battery-powered strobe (Godox AD200Pro / AD300Pro) with a 36" collapsible softbox or beauty dish for fill light against sunset backlighting.
- Support: Heavy-duty light stand with C-stand sandbag weight for stability against wind.
- Accessories: Wireless flash trigger, high-speed sync (HSS) enabled.

## Studio Client Workflow & Deliverables
1. Pre-shoot Consultation: Review mood board, shot list, lighting preferences, and contract agreement.
2. Production: Execute shot list, manage tethered capture into Capture One or Lightroom.
3. Post-production: Color grading, high-resolution retouching, online client gallery delivery within 5 business days.
