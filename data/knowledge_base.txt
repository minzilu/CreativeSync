### 21. Location Logistics and Specifics for Shoots (Comprehensive Guide)

Colombo & Suburbs:
- Viharamahadevi Park: Free for general, LKR 2000 for commercial shoots. Best time: 06:30 AM - 08:30 AM. Prior permission required for large setups.
- Independence Square: Free. Best time: 06:00 AM - 07:30 AM. No drones allowed. High security zone.
- Galle Face Green: Free. Best time: 05:00 PM - 06:30 PM. Great for sunsets, heavy crowds on weekends.
- Port City Colombo: Free for casual, commercial permit required. Best time: 04:30 PM - 06:30 PM.
- Mount Lavinia Beach: Free (public areas). Best time: 05:00 PM - 06:30 PM. Ideal for golden hour and sunset portraits.
- Bellanwila Park: Free. Best time: 06:00 AM - 08:00 AM. Good for fitness and casual lifestyle shoots.
- Seema Malakaya & Beira Lake: Entrance fee LKR 300-500. Best time: 06:30 AM - 08:00 AM. Temple rules apply (modest dress).

Malabe & Kaduwela:
- Water's Edge Boardwalk: Fee LKR 5,000 Permit. Best time: 05:00 PM - 06:30 PM. Needs advance booking.
- Ape Gama (Battaramulla): Fee LKR 3,000 Permit. Best time: 08:00 AM - 10:00 AM. Traditional village aesthetic.
- Diyatha Uyana: Free. Best time: 06:30 AM - 08:30 AM. Avoid evenings due to crowds.
- Beddagana Wetland Park: Fee LKR 100/200. Best time: 06:30 AM - 09:00 AM. Drones strictly prohibited (bird sanctuary).

Kandy & Central Province:
- Royal Botanical Gardens, Peradeniya: Fee LKR 2,000 for wedding/commercial. Best time: 08:00 AM - 10:30 AM. Advance booking recommended.
- Kandy Lake Round: Free. Best time: 06:30 AM - 08:00 AM. Beautiful backdrop of the Temple of the Tooth.
- Udawatta Kele Sanctuary: Fee LKR 100-200. Best time: 07:00 AM - 09:00 AM. Great for moody, forest-themed shoots.
- Hanthana Mountain Range: Free. Best time: 06:00 AM - 09:00 AM. Requires hiking; prepare for windy and misty conditions.
- Ambuluwawa Tower (Gampola): Ticket required. Best time: 08:00 AM - 10:00 AM. Drones require special permission; can be very windy.

Nuwara Eliya & Ella:
- Lake Gregory: Fee LKR 200 + Camera Fee. Best time: 07:00 AM - 09:30 AM. Misty morning vibes.
- Hakgala Botanical Garden: Fee LKR 1,500 - 2,000. Best time: 08:30 AM - 11:00 AM. Avoid rainy days.
- Nine Arches Bridge, Ella: Free. Best time: 06:00 AM - 08:00 AM. Check train schedules for dynamic background shots.
- Horton Plains National Park: High entrance fee + vehicle fee. Best time: 06:00 AM - 09:00 AM. Strict plastic ban.

Galle & Deep South:
- Galle Dutch Fort: Free for public areas. Best time: 04:00 PM - 06:30 PM. Drone flying requires civil aviation clearance.
- Unawatuna Beach: Free. Best time: 06:00 AM - 08:00 AM. Palm tree swings available for a small fee.
- Mirissa Coconut Tree Hill: Free. Best time: 06:00 AM - 07:30 AM. Extremely crowded during sunset; morning is mandatory for clean shots.
- Jungle Beach, Rumassala: Free. Best time: 07:00 AM - 09:00 AM. Requires a small hike down.
- Matara Pigeon Island Bridge: Free. Best time: 05:30 PM - 06:30 PM. Great for dramatic sunset fill light.
- Polhena Beach: Free. Best time: 06:00 AM - 08:00 AM. Shallow waters, good for couple shoots.

Cultural Triangle & North Western:
- Sigiriya / Pidurangala Rock: Ticket required. Best time: 05:30 AM (Sunrise at Pidurangala) or 04:00 PM. Strenuous climb; pack light gear.
- Negombo Beach Park: Free. Best time: 05:00 PM - 06:30 PM. Wide sandy beaches with catamarans in the background.
